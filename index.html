Business Intelligence

Key Insights & Recommendations

📺
TV is the #1 Sales Driver
TV advertising has the strongest correlation with Sales (r = 0.782) and accounts for ~78% of the Random Forest model's predictive power. Every $1K increase in TV budget adds approximately $0.054K to Sales.
Recommendation: Prioritise TV as the primary advertising channel and never underspend here.
📻
Radio is the Most Cost-Efficient Channel
Radio shows solid correlation (r = 0.576) and is statistically significant (p < 0.001). Radio budgets are far smaller than TV but still deliver meaningful sales uplift, making it the highest ROI channel per dollar spent.
Recommendation: Use Radio as a cost-effective complement to TV — run both simultaneously for a multiplier effect.
📰
Newspaper Budget Should Be Reduced
Newspaper has the weakest correlation (r = 0.228), contributes only ~4% of feature importance, and is statistically not significant in the OLS regression (p = 0.86). High newspaper spend does not meaningfully drive sales.
Recommendation: Cut Newspaper budget and reallocate funds to TV or Radio for higher returns.
🚀
High TV Spend Yields Disproportionate Returns
Products in the "Very High TV" category (>$225K budget) average significantly higher sales with no visible diminishing returns in this dataset's range. Increasing TV spend from medium to high produces a clear step-change in sales.
Recommendation: Do not cap TV budget prematurely — the data supports continued investment.
💡
Combined TV + Radio Strategy is Optimal
The "High Budget" scenario with strong TV and Radio spend predicts the highest sales of all 6 scenarios tested. Running both channels simultaneously likely creates a combined audience-reach effect that outperforms either channel alone.
Recommendation: Allocate the majority of budget to TV (~70%), supplement with Radio (~25%), and minimise Newspaper (~5%).
